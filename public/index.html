About the author

Meet Nuzo.

A pioneering voice in African horror, Nuzo Onoh draws on folklore, ancestral memory and the supernatural to tell stories that linger long after the final page.

Recipient of the Bram Stoker Award for Lifetime Achievement, her work has been published internationally and continues to push the boundaries of contemporary horror.
